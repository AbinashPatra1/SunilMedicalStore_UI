This policy explains what personal information the Sunil Medical Store app ("the App") collects when you use it to browse medicines, book lab tests and doctor appointments, upload prescriptions, and place orders for delivery, and how that information is used, stored, and shared.

Who this policy covers

Sunil Medical Store ("we", "us", "our") operates the App for its retail pharmacy customers. This policy applies to anyone who creates an account and uses the App — it does not cover information collected through any other website or offline interaction with the store.

Information we collect

CategoryWhat we collect, and why
Account & identity Your name and phone number, used to create and sign in to your account via SMS one-time-password verification. We do not set or store a password.
Profile details (optional) Gender, date of birth, and email address, if you choose to add them, used to personalize your account.
Medical information Prescription images you upload, used so our pharmacist can verify and dispense prescription-only medicines; and any medical records associated with your account, shown to you read-only inside the App.
Delivery location Saved delivery addresses, and — only if you grant location permission — your device's GPS coordinates, used once to fill in an address automatically and to confirm your address is inside our delivery area. We do not track your location in the background.
Orders & bookings Your order history, lab test bookings, and doctor appointments — including items ordered, scheduled dates and times, and status — used to fulfill and let you track your requests.
Payment information If you pay online, payment is processed by Razorpay; we receive and store a payment reference and status, never your full card or bank details. If you save a UPI ID for convenience, we store only that ID. Cash-on-delivery orders involve no online payment data.
Notifications A device push-notification token, used to send you order, appointment, and lab-test updates and reminders. You can disable this in your device settings at any time.
Technical & diagnostic data Standard request logs (such as timestamps and error details) generated automatically when the App communicates with our servers, used to keep the service reliable and secure.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify your identity by phone.
  • To process and deliver orders, lab test bookings, and doctor appointments.
  • To verify prescriptions for medicines that require one.
  • To confirm your delivery address is within our serviceable area and calculate delivery charges.
  • To send you order, appointment, and booking status updates and reminders.
  • To respond to support requests and resolve issues with your account or orders.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the App, under terms that restrict them to the purposes below:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — phone-number authentication, prescription-image storage, and push notifications.
  • Razorpay — processing online payments. Razorpay has its own privacy policy governing the payment details you enter in its checkout.
  • Our backend hosting provider — our order/catalog database and API run on cloud infrastructure that stores the information described above on our behalf.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our customers, staff, or the public.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active, and afterward for as long as needed to meet accounting, legal, or dispute-resolution requirements. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time using the contact details below, subject to records we're required to keep by law (for example, completed-order records for tax purposes).

Your choices

  • Edit or review your profile any time from Profile > Account.
  • Manage saved addresses and payment methods from Profile > Addresses / Payment Methods — add, edit, or delete them directly.
  • Turn off location access in your device settings; the App will fall back to manual address entry.
  • Turn off notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data, or close your account entirely, by contacting us (below).

Children's privacy

The App is intended for use by adults managing their own or their family's healthcare needs. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections between the App and our servers and access controls on our backend systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and to respond promptly if an issue arises.

Changes to this policy
